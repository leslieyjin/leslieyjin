Hi, I'm Leslie.

Statistician, researcher, and developer.

I enjoy working across disciplines to turn complex data into insights that people can actually understand. I’m particularly interested in interpretable machine learning, exploratory data analysis, and data visualization, especially for evolving data over time and/or space. I let data speak for itself, and I help people understand what it is saying. My analysis leads to the right questions to ask and the appropriate strategies to use. As a developer, I also love building tools and visualizations that make data more intuitive—and, ideally, a little more fun to explore.
