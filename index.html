Research Statement

Hi, I'm Leslie.

Statistician, researcher, and developer.

I am currently a Postdoctoral Fellow at National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences. I enjoy working with data from various disciplines, translating complex data into languages that people can actually understand. These experiences also motivated my own research, which focuses interpretable machine learning, exploratory data analysis, and software development. In the big data era, most datasets are under-explored. I want to discover from these massive resources what other questions we could ask, what other stories the data is telling us. I make methods that make these messages clear and software to make these information accessible.
