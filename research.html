Interdisciplinary collaboration

I enjoy working with a variety of data! I have collaborated with domain experts in epidemiology, toxicology, neurobiology, clinical research and even finance and economics! Currently, I am particularly interested in massive observational data with complex structure, such as Electronic Health Records (EHR), wearable devices and sensors. I believe these massive datasets are underexplored, and I am excited to discover something new!

Interpretable machine learning/AI

We've heard so much about black-box models. How about glass-box models? Well, to be honest, it would be more like a "frosted glass-box" models. It might be difficult to fully understand complicated ML models like AI. However, interpretation methods can give us some insights into how these models work. It at least can tell use what features models deem important, and how these features interact with each other. With these understandings we no longer need to blindly trust the models. It help us avoid bias, improve transparency and tune the models more efficiently!

Data visualization

Graphics are the most powerful tools to communicate information. I love making concise, comprehensive graphical representations of big data and models. I am especially interested in visualizing dynamic system, such as temporal and/or spacial changes. Evolving graphics can reveal information that cannot be seen in cross-sectional or aggregated summaries!
